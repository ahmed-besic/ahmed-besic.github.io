About Media Compression

This tool allows you to compress video and audio files directly in your browser. No files are uploaded to any server - all processing happens locally on your device.

Features

  • Compress videos and audio files to a target size
  • Convert between different media formats
  • Adjust quality settings for better compression
  • 100% client-side processing for privacy

Supported Formats

Video: MP4, WebM, MOV, AVI

Audio: MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC

Note: Compression quality and speed depend on your device's processing power. Larger files may take longer to process.
