About BMI

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a numerical value derived from a person's weight and height. It provides a simple rule of thumb for classifying a person's weight as underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese.

BMI is not a direct measurement of body fat and doesn't account for factors like muscle mass, bone density, or overall body composition.
